- **Macro:** Middle East conflict referenced as a probable driver (your input). Plausible — UK consumer spending tends to soften during sustained geopolitical uncertainty. Effect would be cross-category, which fits (kits, colours, remove all -22% to -40%).

**Most likely:** combination of all three. Easter normalisation is the structural driver; macro tone amplifies it; transition adds noise. W18 is the tell — if it tracks 75-90/d through this week, normalisation. If it stays at <70/d through Friday, structural.

### Liquid Repurchase Ratio (key insight)

| Window | Liquid DSR | Kit DSR | Ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 30d | 35.8 | 81.9 | 0.44 |
| 14d | 47.0 | 72.0 | 0.65 |
| 7d | 42.8 | 64.2 | **0.67** |

**Liquid-to-kit ratio up 49% in 14d (0.44 → 0.65).** Customers are buying standalone liquids at a much higher rate relative to kit purchases. Two readings:

1. **Positive:** existing customers refilling — strong retention signal even as new-kit acquisition softens.
2. **Mechanical:** kits are dropping faster than liquids, so the ratio rises by deflation — not because more reorders, but because fewer first-times.

The Base spike (+75% in 7d) is the dominant liquid driver. **If Base alone is causing the ratio shift, it's a Base-specific signal not a retention signal.** Strip Base out and total liquid DSR drops to ~22.5/d (7d) vs ~24/d (30d) = roughly flat. So **the repurchase improvement is essentially Base. Worth understanding what changed.**

Cross-checked #sale-announcements: no Base promo, no UK promo in W16-W17. If you can't find a CRO change either, candidates are:
- Organic re-order cycle hitting (Base typically used 1.5-2x faster than other liquids)
- A creator/influencer mention or ad creative change
- Customer stockpiling ahead of perceived shortage (unlikely — they wouldn't know)
- Pricing change on the standalone PDP

The reason for the Base lift is unexplained from the data we have: not promo-driven, not CRO-driven (per your check), not aligned with kit volume (kits are dropping). Worth a Shopify-side investigation when bandwidth allows.

5. **Catalogue is healthy** — 79 colours generating 90% of volume, broad-shouldered tail. **39 dead colour SKUs** sitting idle (1,194 units of those are Christmas-themed) — Marketing call on whether to clear or hold for Q4 relaunch.

6. **Cotton (POW-COT-030) and Remove 120ml (ACC-REM)** both flagged for sharp drops likely tied to listing health. Worth Daniel / CX-side check before they show up in W18 numbers.
